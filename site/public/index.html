AdPocket

Your ad revenue, in your pocket. AdPocket is a mobile app for Android and iOS for app and website publishers. It shows today's revenue, trends and full reports from Yandex Advertising Network and Google AdMob on your phone and home-screen widget. It only reads statistics and has no server: everything stays on your device.

Purpose of the app

AdPocket lets publishers check the earnings of their own advertising accounts on their phone. It is a read-only viewer: when you connect Google AdMob, AdPocket uses your permission only to download your own AdMob reports (earnings, impressions, clicks, requests) and display them to you. It does not create, edit or share content, does not use Google data for advertising or AI, and is free to use without an AdPocket account. The website is public and needs no login.

What it does

Today at a glance

Revenue, impressions, clicks and eCPM for today, with yesterday, the last 7 days and the month for comparison.

Full reports

Any period, metrics, groupings and filters, with totals, charts, sorting and CSV export.

Home-screen widgets for Android and iOS and optional local notifications with your daily revenue.

Optional Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode lock.

No server, no tracking

No sign-up, no analytics, no ads. Statistics go straight from Yandex or Google to your device.

Connecting your accounts

Yandex Advertising Network

Paste the free read-only Statistics API token from partner.yandex.ru. It is kept in the device's secure storage.

Google AdMob

Tap "Sign in with Google" and allow read-only access to your AdMob reports. Then pick the AdMob account to show.

How AdPocket uses Google user data

  • What it asks for: your Google account's e-mail address (to label the account) and the admob.readonly scope, which is read-only access to your AdMob reports, apps and account list. AdPocket cannot change anything in AdMob.
  • What it does with it: it requests your AdMob network and mediation reports (earnings, impressions, clicks, requests and related metrics) and shows them in the app and its widget. That is the only use.
  • Where it goes: requests go directly from your device to Google's AdMob API. Results are cached on your device only. AdPocket has no server, and no Google data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • How to stop it: remove the AdMob account in the app (Settings → Accounts), which also revokes AdPocket's access. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AdPocket's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the privacy policy.

Рекламная сеть Яндекса

Вставьте бесплатный OAuth‑токен API статистики (только чтение) из partner.yandex.ru. Он хранится в защищённом хранилище устройства.

Google AdMob

Нажмите «Войти через Google» и разрешите доступ к отчётам AdMob только на чтение. Затем выберите аккаунт AdMob.

Как AdPocket использует данные Google

  • Что запрашивается: адрес e‑mail Google‑аккаунта (чтобы подписать аккаунт в приложении) и разрешение admob.readonly: доступ только на чтение к отчётам, приложениям и списку аккаунтов AdMob. AdPocket ничего не может изменить в AdMob.
  • Зачем: чтобы запрашивать отчёты AdMob (сеть и медиация: доход, показы, клики, запросы и связанные показатели) и показывать их в приложении и виджете. Других целей нет.
  • Куда передаются: запросы идут напрямую с устройства в AdMob API Google. Результаты кэшируются только на устройстве. У AdPocket нет сервера; данные Google не передаются разработчику или кому‑либо ещё.
  • Как отключить: удалите аккаунт AdMob в приложении («Настройки → Аккаунты»), при этом доступ AdPocket отзывается. Также доступ можно отозвать на myaccount.google.com/permissions.
