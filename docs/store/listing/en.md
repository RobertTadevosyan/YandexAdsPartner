## Full description (Google Play 4000 / App Store)
AdPocket puts the Yandex Advertising Network partner dashboard in your pocket. Open the app and see in seconds how your sites and apps are earning today, spot trends, build any report the web interface can, and get a summary every morning — all without a laptop.

OVERVIEW
• Today's revenue with the change versus yesterday
• Yesterday, last 7 days, this month and last month, each with its comparison
• 30-day revenue and impressions chart
• eCPM, fill rate and CTR for the period you pick
• Top apps and sites by revenue, one tap away from a detailed report

REPORTS
• Main, Mobile Mediation, SSP and DSP statistics
• Period presets or any custom date range
• Metrics, groupings (by day, week, month, geography, app, block, OS, browser…) and filters — the same catalogue as partner.yandex.ru
• Totals, sortable table, chart view, saved presets, CSV export

WIDGETS AND NOTIFICATIONS
• Home-screen widget with today's revenue, trend and a refresh button
• Daily and monthly summaries at the time you choose
• Alerts when revenue drops or impressions stop — all generated on your device, no push servers

ACCOUNTS AND SECURITY
• Several YAN accounts side by side
• Tokens are stored in the system secure store (Keystore / Keychain)
• Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or device PIN
• Russian and English interface, currency and VAT settings, light and dark themes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
AdPocket has no server, no analytics and no ads. Your OAuth token and your statistics stay on your device and are sent only to Yandex's API.

HOW TO SIGN IN
Open partner.yandex.ru, press the "API" button on the right panel, choose "Get OAuth token for the Statistics API" and paste the token into the app. The app shows these steps on its first screen.
